About the group

Tension, consequence, character.

We tell stories where things go wrong. From the cold void of Mothership to the creeping dread of Call of Cthulhu, from the fragile heroism of Mouse Guard to the folkloric shadows of Vaesen, and the perilous roads of Dungeons & Dragons, our table lives in the space where tension, consequence, and character collide.

Coming soon: immersive whodunnit-style roleplay experiences, mystery games, and other tabletop adventures designed to pull everyone into the story.
