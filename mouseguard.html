Mouse Guard

Mouse Guard is a roleplaying game of brave mice protecting their communities in a dangerous and ever-changing world. As members of the Mouse Guard, players patrol the wilds, guide travelers, defend the territories, and face the weather, predators, and other threats that make even a short journey an adventure. It is a game about courage, duty, friendship, and the small heroes who keep the world safe.
